I bridge business, technology and operations through practical software projects. My technical base is full-stack development; my direction is project coordination and digitalization.

## Selected work

### Pilares - daily habits in one place

Daily habits and reflections are often spread across separate tools. Pilares brings five areas of life into one bilingual app, with core progress stored in the browser and no account needed to start.

I define the product direction and priorities, including small daily actions, language support and clear limits on data storage. The live beta shows the five spaces and habit controls. Local data has no automatic backup or cross-device sync.

[Try Pilares](https://pilares.pages.dev/)

### Ponte - a job-search organizer

A job search needs a clear view of applications and next steps without moving personal records into another database. Ponte works with Google Sheets selected by the user.

My contribution is the workflow and privacy limits: application status and notes can be edited, while contact records stay read-only. The public repository documents the architecture, data contract and tests with invented data. The live interface is available; real-account sign-in and save flows remain under validation.

[View the live interface](https://ponte-vat.pages.dev/) · [Source and process](https://github.com/iurjoh/ponte)
